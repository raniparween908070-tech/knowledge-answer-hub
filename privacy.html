Privacy Policy

We value transparency in how Knowledge Answer Hub handles your questions, contact submissions, and basic technical data.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect and Process

Knowledge Answer Hub is designed so visitors can ask educational questions without creating an account or registering a profile. However, operating a web application requires processing certain information provided directly by you or generated during your connection:

  • Questions and Follow-Up Prompts: Text you enter into the question form, along with your selected subject category, language preference, and in-session follow-up conversation context.
  • Contact Form Submissions: Your name, email address, subject, message content, and submission timestamp when you choose to send a message through our Contact Us page.
  • Technical and Security Metadata: Standard server connection metadata such as IP address (used temporarily in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention) and standard HTTP request headers logged by web hosting infrastructure.

2. How Questions Submitted by Visitors Are Handled

When you submit a question on the homepage, your browser sends the request to our Node.js backend server. Our server validates the input, applies rate-limiting checks, and forwards the question, selected subject, and language preference to the Google Gemini API to generate an answer.

We do not publish visitor questions on public web pages, and we do not maintain a public archive of user prompts. Please avoid entering sensitive personal details, passwords, government identification numbers, or confidential medical and financial records into the question box.

3. Contact Form Information

When you submit our Contact Us form, the details you provide (name, email address, subject, and message) are received through the form-handling service of our hosting provider (Netlify), where they are stored privately and may be forwarded to our site administrators by email so they can read and respond to your inquiry. Contact messages are never published publicly or sold to third parties.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Knowledge Answer Hub does not set advertising cookies or third-party behavioral tracking cookies. Any conversation thread you view on the homepage exists only in your current browser session memory and is cleared when you click Clear or reload the page. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

5. Third-Party AI Services (Google Gemini)

To generate answers, Knowledge Answer Hub sends your submitted question and session follow-up context to the Google Gemini API from our backend server. Because Google Gemini is an external third-party AI service, your prompt text is processed by Google's servers to produce the response in accordance with Google's API terms and privacy policies. API credentials remain strictly on our server and are never exposed to visitor browsers.

6. Data Handling and Retention

We retain data only for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Question Prompts: Processed in memory during the API request and not persisted in a user profile database by our application.
  • Rate-Limiting Records: IP-based request counters are kept temporarily in server memory during short rolling windows (minutes) to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Contact Messages: Stored in our hosting provider's private form inbox until reviewed, resolved, or deleted upon request.

7. Security Practices

We apply practical technical safeguards to protect user interactions and stored messages, including HTTP security headers, input length validation and sanitization, endpoint rate limiting, strict server-side storage of API keys in protected hosting settings, and spam filtering on the contact form. While no internet transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security, we continually maintain our software to reduce risk.

8. Your Rights and Contact Information

Depending on your region, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal contact details you previously submitted through our Contact Us form. To make a privacy inquiry or request deletion of a contact message, please reach out via our Contact Us page and reference the email address and approximate date of your message.
